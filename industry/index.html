How it works

One problem, one semester

The firm picks the problem. The team does the research. The firm sees the result, and the people who produced it.

01 · ScopeThe firm brings one problem from its prediction-market work and agrees with us on the data, the deliverable, and what a good result looks like.
02 · BuildA small team of members works on it through the semester, checking in regularly with researchers at the firm.
03 · PresentAt the end of the semester the team hands over its code and a written result, and presents it to the firm.
04 · InterviewMembers whose work stands out go straight to the firm's internship or full-time interviews.
Problems

What a project can be

Anything a trading firm wants answered about event contracts. Some examples, drawn from questions our members already work on:

Pricing

Cross-venue pricing

The same event trades on Kalshi, Polymarket and other venues. When do their prices disagree, and does the gap survive fees and execution?

Calibration

Are prices probabilities?

How far a market's prices sit from realized outcome frequencies, and whether that wedge can be traded or corrected.

Liquidity

Market making in event contracts

Quoting binary contracts: spreads, inventory, and adverse selection as an event approaches resolution.

Macro

Scheduled releases

Contracts on FOMC decisions, CPI prints and earnings, against the futures and options that hedge the same outcome.

Sports

In-game pricing

Live win-probability models against the market, tick by tick, and where the market lags the game.

Forecasting

Forecasting systems

Statistical and LLM agents that forecast resolutions using only the information available at the time.
