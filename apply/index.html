      Questions we get
      

        
Do I need to know anything about prediction markets?
No. If you can read a probability and argue about it, that's the bar.

        
What do members actually work with?
Public data — order books, price histories, and resolution records that prediction-market venues publish over open APIs.

        
What's the time commitment?
About three hours a week: one meeting, plus whatever your project needs that week.

        
Is there a deadline?
Rolling. Applications are reviewed weekly, and you can join mid-semester.

        
Can graduate students join?
Yes. Every market is open to graduate students.
